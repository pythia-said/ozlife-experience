# Feature overview

[Back to the showcase](../README.md)

This page describes publicly suitable product capabilities at a high level. It intentionally excludes implementation details, proprietary content collections, internal identifiers, and complete data structures.

## Daily

- A central Daily Hub with a chosen focus, daily guidance, Mind, Body, and Energy tasks, progress, onboarding support, rewards, and weekly context
- An atmospheric focus ritual for choosing what the day needs
- A daily three-part loop with completion feedback
- XP, streaks, focus progress, milestones, daily rewards, and 28-day runs

## Mind

- A daily brain game and a broader game catalogue
- Favorites for returning to preferred activities
- Practical mind tips and neutral reflection prompts
- Breathing and rest practices

## Body

- Short workouts and guided sessions
- Exercise media, filters, and progress tracking
- Four structured 28-day paths
- A personal questionnaire that can shape a routine around goals, experience level, preferred duration, environment, and recovery needs

## Energy

- An Energy Compass and personal check-ins
- Guided mini-sessions
- Lessons related to finance and everyday life
- Reflection on the perceived effect of an activity
- Weekly review, pattern awareness, and small experiments

## Diary

- Categorized journal entries
- Neutral reflection prompts
- Voice-note support
- A library for favorites and saved content
- Optional biometric protection for private areas

## Nutrition

- Nutrition basics
- A recipe collection and recipe details
- Shopping-list support

## Garage

- Avatars, outfit sets, and collectible items
- Unlock progression, badges, and visual rewards
- Daily Garage activities that connect consistency with visible progress

## Apple integrations

OZLife can use selected Apple platform capabilities where appropriate:

- Optional Apple Health access
- Notifications
- Audio playback
- Sharing
- Biometric protection
- A home-screen widget

Optional integrations remain subject to user choice and platform permission.

## Across the experience

- A free daily foundation with optional premium depth, including broader access to programs, games, insights, and selected rewards
- Content structures for German, English, Spanish, and Italian

The product is in active pre-release development. Features may evolve as release validation, accessibility work, rights review, and product refinement continue.
