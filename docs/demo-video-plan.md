# Demo video plan

[Back to the showcase](../README.md)

## Purpose

Create a calm **15–25 second** product glimpse that communicates the daily OZLife rhythm without presenting an unverified release state. The video should complement the screenshot story rather than replace it.

No video or source media is currently included in this repository.

## Suggested flow

| Time | Moment | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 seconds | App start or Daily Hub | Establish OZLife and the day at a glance |
| 3–6 seconds | Choose the daily focus | Introduce Mind, Body, and Energy |
| 6–11 seconds | Open one Mind, Body, or Energy action | Show that the next step is small and approachable |
| 11–15 seconds | Show progress or a neutral reflection moment | Make the effect and continuity visible |
| 15–20 seconds | Show the Garage or another visual reward | Connect consistency with tangible progress |
| Final 2–3 seconds | OZLife brand frame and claim | End quietly and clearly |

Transitions should be restrained. Use enough time for each screen to be understood, and avoid rapid feature montages.

## Recommended format

- **Primary aspect ratio:** 9:16 portrait
- **Primary resolution:** 1,080 × 1,920 pixels
- **Frame rate:** 24 or 30 frames per second
- **Container and codec:** MP4 with H.264 video
- **Audio:** optional; use AAC only when every sound, voice, and music right is cleared
- **Duration:** 15–25 seconds
- **Repository-friendly size:** aim for **10 MB or less**; treat **25 MB** as an editorial upper ceiling and recompress before exceeding it

Prepare a static, approved poster frame for environments where inline playback is unavailable or undesirable.

## Capture and editorial guidance

- Use a production-configured presentation with developer controls hidden.
- Use synthetic or empty personal content.
- Keep taps, scrolls, and transitions slow enough to follow.
- Avoid unverified prices, unfinished purchase states, launch language, and App Store claims.
- Keep the final claim consistent: **Small daily routines for Body, Mind & Energy.**
- Preserve readable contrast and avoid flashing or unnecessarily fast motion.
- Add captions for any spoken content and provide a concise text description near the final embed.

## Content safety review

Before publication, inspect every frame and the audio track for:

- Real diary entries, voice notes, health information, notifications, or personal data
- Names, account identifiers, device names, timestamps, or location details
- Debug overlays, developer menus, diagnostics, internal labels, or test states
- Internal URLs, infrastructure, configuration, signing, purchase, or entitlement identifiers
- Secrets, tokens, keys, certificates, or credentials
- Unapproved artwork, recognizable likenesses, voices, fonts, music, or third-party material
- Metadata that should not be included in the public derivative

Do not publish the video until ownership, consent, redistribution rights, product-state accuracy, and privacy have all been reviewed.
