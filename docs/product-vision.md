# Product vision

## A smaller starting point

OZLife is built on a simple idea: meaningful routines are easier to begin when the next step feels manageable.

Many approaches to wellbeing begin with ambitious plans and extensive tracking. OZLife begins with a small action that can fit into an ordinary day. It connects moments of focus, movement, reflection, and everyday awareness without asking people to change everything at once.

## Daily Snacks

OZLife calls these small, repeatable actions **Daily Snacks**. Each one is intended to be approachable on its own while contributing to a broader daily rhythm.

A day can include:

- A Mind activity for focus, rest, breathing, or reflection
- A Body activity for movement or practical nutrition
- An Energy activity for noticing attention, stress, time, money, or everyday patterns

The aim is not to fill every available minute. It is to make a useful next action easier to see and easier to repeat.

## Mind, Body, and Energy

The three product areas are distinct but connected:

- **Mind** supports focus, reflection, rest, and playful cognitive exercise.
- **Body** supports movement through guided workouts, structured paths, and practical nutrition.
- **Energy** supports awareness of the everyday conditions and patterns that shape how a day feels.

Together, they provide a broad general-wellness perspective without presenting any one area as a complete answer.

## Progress without pressure

OZLife makes consistency visible through daily progress, streaks, milestones, 28-day paths, and visual rewards. These elements are intended to support return and recognition, not to turn personal wellbeing into a contest.

The desired experience is steady and supportive: choose what matters today, take a small step, notice its effect, and continue when the next day arrives.

OZLife is a general wellness product. It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
