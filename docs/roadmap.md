# Roadmap

[Back to the showcase](../README.md)

This roadmap is a non-binding statement of product direction. It does not announce release dates, guaranteed features, or App Store availability. Priorities may change as OZLife progresses through pre-release development.

## Near term

- Complete real-device launch quality assurance and production purchase testing
- Produce a sanitized English screenshot set for the public showcase
- Complete rights and provenance review for every visual and audio asset considered for publication
- Refine tablet layouts and accessibility validation
- Make the Library and Diary easier to discover through primary navigation

## Product evolution

- Explore deeper Body insights and connections across Mind, Body, and Energy based on meaningful progress
- Expand guided workouts, Energy sessions, recipes, and structured programs
- Strengthen the connection between daily actions, weekly patterns, and longer-term progress
- Add more expressive Garage sets and achievement moments
- Consider a short product video after the screenshot story is stable

## Quality and trust

- Broaden automated user-interface and state-transition testing
- Continue privacy review for health, diary, voice, and feedback experiences
- Introduce any future mood or sentiment features only with explicit consent and a clear privacy foundation
- Continue accessibility, media-rights, localization, and release-readiness review

OZLife remains in active pre-release development. Items above describe areas of intent rather than commitments.
