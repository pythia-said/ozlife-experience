# Privacy and safety

[Back to the showcase](../README.md)

OZLife includes product areas where people may record personal reflections or choose to connect optional Apple platform capabilities. The following principles describe the public product direction at a high level. They are not a complete privacy policy, security specification, or legal guarantee.

## Personal reflection and diary content

Diary entries, reflection prompts, voice notes, favorites, and saved content can be personal. OZLife is designed around on-device experiences for personal content and progress. Public showcase materials must use empty or synthetic examples and must never expose a real person’s entries, recordings, health information, or other private data.

## Optional health integration

Apple Health access is optional. A person should be able to decide whether to connect this integration through the platform permission flow. Health-related information must not be treated as public showcase material.

## Biometric protection

OZLife includes optional biometric protection for private product areas. Biometrics provide a user-controlled access option; this showcase does not claim that any single control creates absolute security.

## Local processing and storage

The product is designed primarily around local, on-device state for progress and personal content. This statement is intentionally limited: it does not describe storage schemas, persistence mechanisms, platform backups, or guarantees beyond the available product-level information.

## User control

Optional Apple integrations, including health access, notifications, and biometric protection, depend on user choice and platform permissions. People should be able to make these choices with clear context about the feature they are enabling.

## General wellness

OZLife supports general wellness, routines, and reflection. It is not a medical device and does not provide or replace professional advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Anyone with medical or mental-health concerns should seek appropriately qualified professional support.

## Public reporting

Do not place sensitive product details or personal information in public GitHub Issues. Follow the coordination process in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) for potential security concerns.
